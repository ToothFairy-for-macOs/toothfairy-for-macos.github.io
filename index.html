ToothFairy

Switch Bluetooth connections with a single click. ToothFairy lets you connect AirPods or any Bluetooth headphones to your Mac effortlessly—no digging through menus.

Menu Bar Simplicity

Click the icon in your menu bar to switch to AirPods. It fills when connected and shows battery status at a glance. Assign unique icons and hotkeys for multiple devices.

Universal Bluetooth Support

Works with AirPods, headphones, speakers, headsets, game controllers, keyboards, mice—any Bluetooth device your Mac supports. (HomePod excluded, as it's not Bluetooth audio.)

Optimal Audio Quality

Forces the high-quality AAC codec for music, video, and games—bypassing macOS's default SCO when the mic isn't needed. Pure listening, no compromises.

Automation and Control

Run custom shell scripts on connect or disconnect. Seamlessly integrate with your workflow.

How It Works

1. Install and Launch
Add ToothFairy to your menu bar. It detects your Bluetooth devices automatically.

2. Assign Devices
Select your AirPods or other headphones. Choose an icon and optional hotkey.

3. One-Click Switch
Click the menu bar icon to connect. See instant status and battery level.

4. Fine-Tune (Optional)
Set audio codec preferences or scripts for connect/disconnect events.

FAQ

Does it work with non-Apple devices?
Yes, any Bluetooth audio device compatible with macOS—headphones, speakers, and more.

What about battery status?
The icon displays it for supported devices like AirPods. Updates in real-time.

Can I use multiple devices?
Absolutely. Customize icons and hotkeys for each one.
